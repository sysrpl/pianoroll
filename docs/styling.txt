UI styling rules
================

Font
- The default font for UI elements is Ubuntu Regular, 10pt.

Buttons
- All buttons are 25px high and at least 75px wide.
- Buttons are 8px from the window border and 8px apart from each other.

Toolbar buttons
- Toolbar buttons are 24px x 24px.
- Toolbar buttons are 4px apart from each other.

Middle transfer buttons (the < and > arrows and the lock between the panes)
- These buttons are 32px x 32px.
- They are 8px apart from each other and 8px from the sides.

Menu separators
- Separators (horizontal rules) in menus are subtle: 50% less visible (50% opacity).

App icon
- resources/icon.png is the program's icon in the taskbar, in menus such as the
  Mint menu, and on the About screen.
